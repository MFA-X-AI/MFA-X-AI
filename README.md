These days I lead AI projects end to end: sitting down with stakeholders, planning and architecting the system, then building and shipping it, from agentic RAG with LLM-as-judge evaluation to full-stack apps in production. 6+ years in industry, published research in speech ML, a postgraduate researcher at IIUM, and a kendoka (1st Kyu) at AI Kendo Club. Always happy to chat about AI, open source, or anything in between.

### What I'm working on

- **[Xircuits](https://github.com/XpressAI/xircuits)**: open-source visual programming platform for AI workflows. Lead developer since 2021; published 49 of its 51 releases.
- **[Xaibo](https://github.com/XpressAI/xaibo)**: XpressAI's open-source modular agent framework. Added AG-UI structured response events, streaming through the observability proxy, and a chat interface.
- **[Kendo Nexus](https://kendonexus.com)**: community management platform and Android app for kendo clubs and the Malaysia Kendo Association: member portal, club management, gradings, and tournaments with a live shiai scorekeeper.
- **D'AI**: agentic AI framework that answers from a multi-layer knowledge base (SQL, vector search and a knowledge graph) instead of model memory. My master's research: across 15 LLMs it raises exact-match accuracy from 31–69% to 80–99.6% while cutting cost 167x. Silver, IIUM Festival of Ideas 2025.
- **[EMOS → Edge-AI depression screening](https://kerice.fahreza.dev)**: speech emotion recognition and depression detection, carried from my final-year project to my M.Sc. thesis (1st Runner Up, Innovate Malaysia 2018 Google Track; Gold, PECIPTA 2019). Today it's an offline, speech-based depression screening device on a Raspberry Pi for community clinics. Gold Award, KERICE 2026.
